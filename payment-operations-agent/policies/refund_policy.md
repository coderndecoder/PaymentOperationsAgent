# Refund Policy

## Overview
Our refund policy is designed to be fair to customers while protecting the business from abuse.
All refund decisions are made based on the rules below. Support agents must follow these rules exactly
and must not approve refunds outside of these conditions.

## Eligibility Rules

### Rule 1: Time Window
- Refunds are only eligible if the payment was made **within the last 30 days**.
- Payments older than 30 days are **not eligible** for a refund under any circumstance.

### Rule 2: Payment Status
- Only payments with status **"completed"** are eligible for a refund.
- Payments with status **"failed"**, **"pending"**, or **"refunded"** are **not eligible**.
  - "failed" payments were never settled, so no charge occurred.
  - "pending" payments are still being processed — wait for completion first.
  - "refunded" payments have already been refunded and cannot be refunded again.

### Rule 3: Subscription Payments
- **Subscription payments are not eligible for refunds** once the billing cycle has started.
- If a customer cancels before the next billing cycle, future charges will be stopped, but the current period will not be refunded.

### Rule 4: One Refund Per Payment
- Each payment can only be refunded once.
- Partial refunds are not supported at this time.

## Refund Process
1. Support agent verifies eligibility using the rules above.
2. If eligible, agent submits a refund request through the billing system.
3. Refund is processed within **5–10 business days**.
4. Customer receives an email confirmation once the refund is initiated.

## Exceptions
- Exceptions to the 30-day window may be granted **only by a billing manager** in cases of documented system errors.
- No other exceptions are permitted without manager approval.
