# Chargeback Policy

## Overview
A chargeback is a payment reversal initiated by the customer's bank or card issuer, typically after
the customer disputes a transaction. Chargebacks are different from refunds: they are initiated
externally and can carry fees and penalties for the merchant.

## What Is a Chargeback?
- A chargeback occurs when a customer contacts their bank to dispute a charge.
- The bank investigates and may forcibly reverse the payment.
- Unlike refunds, chargebacks are **not processed by our support team** — they go through the card network.

## Difference Between a Refund and a Chargeback
| Feature          | Refund                        | Chargeback                         |
|------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Initiated by     | Customer via support          | Customer via their bank            |
| Processed by     | Our billing team              | Card network (Visa, Mastercard)    |
| Timeline         | 5–10 business days            | 30–90 days                         |
| Merchant fee     | None                          | $15–$25 per chargeback             |
| Impact on account| None                          | Too many = account suspension risk |

## Customer Guidance
- If a customer reports an **unauthorized transaction**, advise them to:
  1. First, contact our support team to verify the charge.
  2. If the charge is confirmed as unauthorized, a refund will be issued within policy.
  3. If they have already filed a chargeback with their bank, we cannot process a simultaneous refund.

## Chargeback Dispute Window
- Customers typically have **60–120 days** from the transaction date to file a chargeback with their bank.
- Our team has **7–10 business days** to respond to a chargeback dispute with evidence.

## How Support Should Handle Chargeback Inquiries
1. Verify the transaction details using the payment ID.
2. Check if the customer is eligible for a standard refund first — offer that as the faster alternative.
3. If the customer insists on proceeding with the bank, document the case and escalate to the billing team.
4. Never advise a customer to file a chargeback if a standard refund is available.

## Repeated Chargebacks
- Customers with **3 or more chargebacks** in a 12-month period may have their accounts reviewed or suspended.
